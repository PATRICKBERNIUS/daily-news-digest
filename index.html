Concurrently with the underwritten offerings, Rapport intends to enter into a share purchase agreement with a certain investor named therein (the 'Investor”), in a separately negotiated transaction (the 'concurrent private placement” and together with the underwritten offerings, the 'offerings”) pursuant to which Rapport intends to offer and sell, and the Investor intends to purchase shares of Rapport’s common stock at the price per share of common stock in the common stock offering. The sale of shares to the Investor in the proposed concurrent private placement will not be registered as part of the common stock offering. The proposed offerings are subject to market and other conditions, and there can be no assurance as to whether or when the offerings may be completed, or as to the actual size or terms of the offerings.
