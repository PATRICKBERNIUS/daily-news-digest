Trump Hosts State Dinner For Xi Jinping At The White House

Moneycontrol

US President Donald Trump is hosting Chinese President Xi Jinping for a state dinner at the White House following their latest summit in Washington. Xi’s visit marks his first trip to the US capital in more than a decade and represents the second meeting between the two leaders this year. A major focus of their discussions is artificial intelligence (AI), as concerns about its rapid development and potential risks continue to grow globally. Xi stressed that AI development should remain under human control, reflecting calls for safeguards and responsible use of the technology. Beyond AI, the leaders face several contentious issues, including trade disputes, tensions over Taiwan, and the war in Iran. Their meeting comes as Washington and Beijing seek to manage competition between two major economic and technological powers. However, persistent mistrust between the two governments remains a significant obstacle, with experts cautioning that it could limit the possibility of major breakthroughs during the talks.
