Morgan Stanley is out with a report showing the global asset management industry hit a record $159 trillion in 2026, but here's the catch — falling management fees are going to squeeze revenue growth through 2030. That dovetails with a McKinsey report out today estimating AI could add $2.52 trillion annually to the global economy, though McKinsey flags a big caveat — if deployment costs rise by 30 percent, that value pool could shrink by a third, wiping out roughly $850 billion in potential gains. So the message from both firms is essentially the same: AI's upside is real, but it's not free, and the economics are more fragile than the hype suggests.
