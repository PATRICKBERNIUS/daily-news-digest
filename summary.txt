Good morning. Let's get you up to speed on today's developments, and there's a lot moving in AI right now.

The biggest story is around Anthropic, the company behind Claude. As it moves toward becoming the first major AI lab to go public, Anthropic has filed paperwork openly stating that its own technology could pose "catastrophic or existential risk to humanity" — a striking admission from a company racing to IPO. Markets, interestingly, didn't flinch. In fact, news of the blockbuster IPO actually revived investor appetite for AI stocks, helping Korean markets rebound Tuesday, with semiconductor heavyweights leading gains. This ties into a bigger theme this week: the Fed, ECB, and Bank of Japan are all announcing policy decisions between Wednesday and Friday, right as Microsoft, Alphabet, and Amazon report earnings — so expect AI spending and monetization to be under a microscope all week.

Meanwhile, Elon Musk threw some cold water on the AI infrastructure boom, warning that most "neoclouds" — the startups that rent out GPU computing power for AI — won't survive as funding tightens. He estimated over 200 such companies exist, and investors are already starting to short them, suggesting a shakeout is coming in the AI infrastructure layer even as the big players keep raising money.

On the application side, AI is showing up everywhere. In India, PM Modi kicked off a three-day science and tech conclave in Delhi covering AI, biotech, and healthcare innovation. The AI startup Sarvam signed a five-year deal with India's defence ministry to build military AI applications, part of a broader push toward domestic defense tech — which lines up with Apollo Micro Systems, a defense electronics firm, posting a 54% jump in quarterly profit. McKinsey, meanwhile, published research showing agentic AI could cut banks' know-your-customer compliance time by 30 to 70%, though governance concerns and the cost of human oversight are still slowing adoption. And on a lighter note, there was a fun story about someone literally using ChatGPT's computer vision to help find a lost earbud buried in gravel — small, but a nice reminder of how far multimodal AI has come for everyday problems. There's also a piece making the rounds urging corporate boards to move past being merely "impressed" by AI demos and start asking harder questions about real implementation.

In biotech, a UK startup called BioOrbit just completed a notable mission to the International Space Station, using microgravity to manufacture protein crystals that could enable next-generation cancer therapies — a neat example of space infrastructure being repurposed for pharma manufacturing. Genmab is partnering with Incyte on an early-stage trial for a combination treatment targeting mutated metastatic colorectal cancer. Northwest Biotherapeutics is expanding its technical team as it pushes forward with personalized immune therapies for solid tumors, and Kos Biotechnology Partners announced a strategic investment in Golden Age Health to widen global access to rare disease treatments. On the research side, NEC's biotech arm is contributing to a pandemic-preparedness project using AI to design vaccines faster, and there's a more academic study out on how plant cells process DNA from Agrobacterium, which matters because that bacterium is the workhorse tool researchers use to genetically engineer plants.

On the markets and gadgets front, Apple's iPhone 18 Pro is off to a solid start, up 3.1% in global sales during its first three weeks, outperforming last year's launch. Indian equities were choppy Tuesday — early losses were pared back by midday with small-cap stocks surging 2.5%, even as IT and consumer staples stayed weak. And in real estate, G Square Group, India's largest plotted land developer, signed a partnership with JSW One Homes to offer more structured home-building services to its customers.

That's the roundup — plenty of AI money and risk questions swirling at once, solid momentum in biotech manufacturing and partnerships, and markets bracing for a heavy week of central bank and big tech earnings news.
