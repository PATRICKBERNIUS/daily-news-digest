President Trump signed an executive order pushing federal agencies to use the term "super intelligence" instead of "artificial intelligence," arguing the word "artificial" carries an unwanted connotation of fakeness. This played out in real time at a White House summit with top tech executives, where Elon Musk actually caught himself mid-sentence, starting to say "AI" before correcting to "SI." The same event produced a viral moment when Trump jokingly called Google CEO Sundar Pichai a "monster" after signing the order, which immediately lit up social media. Beyond the theatrics, there was substantive news too, Trump announced that a large group of AI company leaders signed a voluntary accord agreeing to internal and external safety reviews of their technology, an attempt to ease public anxiety about AI risks. But Trump also drew a hard line on geopolitics, explicitly rejecting the idea of cooperating with China on AI safety, saying the US needs to protect its technological lead rather than share expertise with Beijing.
